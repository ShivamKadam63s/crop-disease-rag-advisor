## Rice blast
type: disease
aliases: blast; neck blast; leaf blast; Magnaporthe blast
pathogen: Magnaporthe oryzae (fungus)
symptoms: spindle shaped or diamond shaped spots with grey centre and brown edge on leaves; many spots join and whole leaves dry up; neck of the panicle turns black and breaks; panicles stay white and empty; seedlings in nursery get burnt look
favorable_conditions: high humidity, cool nights, long dew period and heavy nitrogen fertilizer
cultural_control: Use resistant varieties and treated seed. Apply nitrogen in split doses and avoid excess. Keep the field and bunds free of weeds and use balanced fertilizer.
biological_control: Seed treatment with Pseudomonas fluorescens can lower infection.
chemical_control: Systemic fungicides such as tricyclazole are commonly used at tillering and booting as per recommendation. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.

## Bacterial leaf blight
type: disease
aliases: BLB; kresek; Xanthomonas blight; bacterial blight of rice
pathogen: Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae (bacterium)
symptoms: water soaked yellow to white stripes start from the leaf tip and edges; stripes turn straw coloured with wavy margin and the leaf dries; milky or yellow bacterial ooze drops on young leaves in the morning; young seedlings wilt and die which is called kresek; disease shows after flooding and storms
favorable_conditions: heavy rain, strong wind, flooded fields, injured leaves and excess nitrogen
cultural_control: Plant resistant varieties and use clean seed. Avoid excess nitrogen and drain the field during severe disease. Avoid clipping seedling tips at transplanting and do not let water flow from infected fields.
biological_control: Seed treatment with Pseudomonas fluorescens may reduce early infection.
chemical_control: Chemical options are limited. Follow local agriculture officer recommendation and the product label.

## Sheath blight
type: disease
aliases: Rhizoctonia sheath blight; banded blight
pathogen: Rhizoctonia solani (fungus)
symptoms: oval greenish grey water soaked spots on the leaf sheath near the water line; spots enlarge with brown border and a snake skin pattern; lesions climb up the plant to the flag leaf; plants in dense patches lodge and have poor grain filling; small round sclerotia float on water in the field
favorable_conditions: dense planting, high nitrogen, high humidity and warm weather
cultural_control: Use wider spacing and avoid excess nitrogen. Remove weeds and crop debris and drain water at times to reduce humidity inside the canopy.
biological_control: Trichoderma or Pseudomonas based products can be used as per recommendation.
chemical_control: Fungicides such as validamycin or hexaconazole are commonly recommended. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.

## Brown spot
type: disease
aliases: Helminthosporium leaf spot; Bipolaris brown spot; sesame leaf spot of rice
pathogen: Bipolaris oryzae (fungus)
symptoms: small oval brown spots with grey centre and dark brown margin on leaves; similar dark spots on the grain husk; seedlings get blighted in nursery; spots look like sesame seeds scattered on leaves; plants in poor soil look weak and spots are many
favorable_conditions: nutrient poor soil especially low potassium or nitrogen, water stress and warm humid weather
cultural_control: Use treated healthy seed and apply balanced fertilizer including potash. Keep proper water in the field and avoid drought stress.
biological_control: Seed treatment with Trichoderma or Pseudomonas products may help.
chemical_control: Protective fungicides such as mancozeb are used as per recommendation. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.
