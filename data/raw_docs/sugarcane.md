## Red rot
type: disease
aliases: Colletotrichum falcatum red rot; red rot of sugarcane
pathogen: Colletotrichum falcatum (fungus)
symptoms: top leaves droop and dry from the leaf edges; reddish dark spots on the midrib of leaves; stalk split lengthwise shows red inside with white cross bands; sour alcohol smell comes from the cut stalk; whole clump dries and stalk becomes hollow and shrunken
favorable_conditions: infected seed pieces, waterlogging, monsoon rains and ratooning of an infected crop
cultural_control: Use disease free seed setts and resistant varieties. Uproot and destroy infected clumps and avoid ratooning an infected field. Treat setts before planting and follow crop rotation.
biological_control: Trichoderma applied to the seed setts and soil can lower the disease.
chemical_control: There is no curative spray. Sett treatment with recommended fungicides is advised. Follow the product label and local recommendation.

## Smut
type: disease
aliases: sugarcane smut; whip smut; Sporisorium scitamineum
pathogen: Sporisorium scitamineum (fungus)
symptoms: a black whip like structure comes out from the top of the shoot; the whip is covered with a silvery thin skin that breaks and releases black powder; plants have many thin grassy tillers; affected canes are thin and stunted; ratoon crops show more whips
favorable_conditions: infected seed setts, dry weather and ratoon crops
cultural_control: Use healthy setts and resistant varieties. Cover the whips with a bag, cut them and destroy them and uproot affected clumps. Hot water treatment of setts is advised.
biological_control: No dependable biological control.
chemical_control: Fungicide treatment of setts is recommended before planting. Follow the product label and local recommendation.
