## Tikka leaf spot
type: disease
aliases: tikka disease; early and late leaf spot; Cercospora leaf spot; groundnut leaf spot
pathogen: Cercospora arachidicola and Nothopassalora personata (fungi)
symptoms: dark brown to black round spots on leaflets; a yellow ring around the spots on the upper surface; spots on the underside look darker; many spots join and leaves dry and drop; severe leaf fall leaves bare stems and pod yield is reduced
favorable_conditions: humid and rainy weather, continuous groundnut and infected crop residue
cultural_control: Rotate with cereals and remove or deep bury crop residue. Use tolerant varieties and treated seed and avoid very dense sowing.
biological_control: Seed treatment with Trichoderma products may reduce early infection.
chemical_control: Protective fungicides such as chlorothalonil or mancozeb are commonly recommended. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.

## Groundnut rust
type: disease
aliases: rust of groundnut; Puccinia arachidis
pathogen: Puccinia arachidis (fungus)
symptoms: orange brown powdery pustules mainly on the underside of leaflets; brown spots show on the upper side of the same leaves; leaves dry up and drop early; rust powder comes off on the hand; spreads quickly in wind from field to field
favorable_conditions: humid weather with dew and wind borne spores from nearby volunteer plants
cultural_control: Use tolerant varieties and remove volunteer groundnut plants. Avoid growing groundnut continuously and sow at the recommended time.
biological_control: No dependable biological control.
chemical_control: Fungicides such as chlorothalonil or triazole products are recommended when rust starts. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.
