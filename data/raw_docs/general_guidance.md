## Pesticide safety
type: guidance
aliases: pesticide safety; spraying safety
pathogen: not applicable
symptoms: not applicable
favorable_conditions: not applicable
cultural_control: Use only pesticides registered for the crop and read the label before every spray. Wear gloves, mask and full clothing, do not spray against the wind, and keep the pre harvest interval written on the label.
biological_control: Prefer scouting, resistant varieties and bio-pesticides where they are recommended.
chemical_control: Doses depend on the product, crop stage and spray volume, so always follow the label of the registered product and the advice of the local agriculture officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra.
