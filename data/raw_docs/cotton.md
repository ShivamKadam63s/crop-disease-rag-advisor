## Pink bollworm
type: pest
aliases: PBW; Pectinophora gossypiella; pink boll worm; gulabi sundi
pathogen: Pectinophora gossypiella (moth larva)
symptoms: flowers do not open fully and look twisted like a rosette; small entry holes on green bolls; pinkish caterpillars inside bolls feeding on seeds; locules are webbed together and cotton lint is stained; bolls open prematurely and seeds are damaged
favorable_conditions: long duration crop, late sowing, continuous cotton and left over crop residue and stalks
cultural_control: Install pheromone traps to monitor moths. Sow on time with short duration varieties and destroy crop residue and stalks after the last picking. Avoid ratoon cotton.
biological_control: Release of egg parasitoid Trichogramma and use of mating disruption are used in integrated management.
chemical_control: Insecticides are used only when pest level crosses the threshold as per local recommendation. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.

## Cotton bacterial blight
type: disease
aliases: angular leaf spot; black arm; Xanthomonas blight of cotton
pathogen: Xanthomonas citri pv. malvacearum (bacterium)
symptoms: angular water soaked spots on leaves limited by the veins that turn brown; spots join and leaves dry and fall; black lesions on the stem and leaf stalk called black arm; dark greasy spots on bolls and boll rot; seedlings may get small spots on seed leaves
favorable_conditions: warm wet weather, rain splash and infected seed or residue
cultural_control: Use acid delinted certified seed and resistant varieties. Destroy infected crop residue and avoid working in the wet field.
biological_control: No dependable biological control.
chemical_control: Copper based sprays are used as per local recommendation. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.
