## Yellow rust
type: disease
aliases: stripe rust; Puccinia striiformis; yellow stripe rust
pathogen: Puccinia striiformis (fungus)
symptoms: yellow to orange powdery pustules arranged in stripes along the leaf veins; yellow powder rubs off on fingers and clothes; leaves turn yellow and dry early; disease appears in patches in the field in cool weather; stripes may also be seen on the ear glumes
favorable_conditions: cool weather around 10 to 15 degrees Celsius with dew, fog or light rain
cultural_control: Grow resistant varieties and scout the field regularly from early stage. Avoid late and excess nitrogen and destroy volunteer wheat plants.
biological_control: No dependable biological control.
chemical_control: Triazole fungicides such as propiconazole are sprayed promptly after the first patches appear. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.

## Leaf rust
type: disease
aliases: brown rust; Puccinia triticina; orange rust of wheat
pathogen: Puccinia triticina (fungus)
symptoms: small round orange brown pustules scattered randomly on the upper surface of leaves; pustules are not in lines like stripe rust; orange powder comes off on touching; heavily infected leaves dry and grain gets shrivelled; disease is seen in warmer weather late in the season
favorable_conditions: moderate warm temperature around 15 to 25 degrees Celsius with leaf wetness
cultural_control: Use resistant varieties and sow at the recommended time. Remove volunteer plants and avoid excess nitrogen.
biological_control: No dependable biological control.
chemical_control: Fungicides such as propiconazole or tebuconazole are used as per recommendation. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.

## Wheat powdery mildew
type: disease
aliases: powdery mildew; Blumeria graminis; white mildew of wheat
pathogen: Blumeria graminis f. sp. tritici (fungus)
symptoms: white to grey powdery fungal growth on leaves and stems that can be wiped off; patches of white powder on lower leaves first; later small black dots appear in the old patches; leaves turn yellow and dry in thick crop; dense lush crop shows more disease
favorable_conditions: cool humid weather, dense crop and high nitrogen
cultural_control: Use resistant varieties and avoid dense sowing and excess nitrogen. Remove weeds and volunteer plants.
biological_control: No dependable biological control.
chemical_control: Wettable sulphur or triazole fungicides are used as per recommendation. Follow the product label for dose and spray interval.

## Karnal bunt
type: disease
aliases: partial bunt; Tilletia indica; kernel bunt
pathogen: Tilletia indica (fungus)
symptoms: some grains in the ear are partly changed into black powder; broken grains smell like rotten fish; only a few grains in an ear are affected and the rest look normal; black powder is seen at the grain furrow and spills on threshing; disease is seen only at harvest and not on the leaves
favorable_conditions: cloudy, rainy and humid weather at flowering and ear emergence
cultural_control: Use certified clean seed and resistant varieties. Avoid irrigation at flowering in cloudy weather and rotate crops.
biological_control: No dependable biological control.
chemical_control: A fungicide spray such as propiconazole at flowering is recommended in affected areas. Follow the product label for dose and timing.
